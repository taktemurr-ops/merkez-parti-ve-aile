Party and Family brings 20 games into one app, all played by passing a single phone around. Teams compete in Forbidden Words, Silent Cinema, Who Am I? and Draw and Guess; Villagers and Vampires and Who’s the Spy? heat up the table with secret roles; Family Quiz and Fast Fingers put four players on one screen. Pick player names, teams, timer, age pack and your own words in the lobby.

• Describe and guess: Forbidden Words, Silent Cinema, Draw and Guess, Who Am I?, Emoji Clues, Broken Pen, Heat Dial
• Secret roles: Villagers and Vampires, Who’s the Spy?
• Race on one screen: Family Quiz, Fast Fingers, Word Bomb, Three in Five, Memory Chain, Name City
• Talk and stories: Truth or Challenge, Spin the Arrow, Most Likely To, Emoji Stories
• Table helper: Scoreboard

Highlights
• 100,000 different decks per game; one phone goes around the table, no internet needed.
• 1–12 players: team mode, timers and "pass the phone" screens so nobody sees another’s card.
• Player names and your own words are saved on the device; age packs pick child-friendly cards.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
